## Design specificity verdict
- Copy is specific to Indian real-estate developers (cost sheets, allotment letters, "locked until Approved or Not Required").
- Visuals are category-interchangeable SaaS: WebGL pastel blob, glass pills, gradient cards with faded corner icons.
- Both main product visuals show the wrong market: hero raster "Acme Corp. Deal / $50,000" with baked-in title; demo is a US invoice ("123 Market St, San Francisco", `$10,355.00`).
- The strongest idea, a document that cannot be downloaded until approved, is never shown.

Detector, code scan: 2 findings (gradient-text Demo.tsx:68; overused-font Fraunces index.css:92, discounted).
Detector, live page: 105 findings; 80 are deliberate mock-invoice micro-text in Demo.tsx (not aria-hidden). Real hits: textarea placeholder low contrast (3.0:1); thin-border/wide-shadow on 10 elements (all glass buttons, nav, hero frame, form card); radial glows on six feature cards and hero; gradient-text h1.

## Priority issues
1. **[P0] Enquiry form fakes success; legal links are dead.** formEndpoint is empty but the form shows "received"; Privacy/Terms/Cookies go to `#`; AppExchange button goes to the generic homepage; a 300-character name overflows; focus is not managed. Fix: never show "sent" without an endpoint (mailto or honest error); privacy line under submit; maxLength and break-words; focus first error and the success message. Command: harden.
2. **[P1] Visuals contradict the audience; Demo breaks the design system.** USD invoice in another site's style (system sans, stock slate, orange-pink gradient, emoji, "done!"); hero visual hidden on mobile. Fix: rebuild the demo in site tokens with an INR cost sheet (Tower B, Unit 1204, milestones) ending "Approved, download unlocked"; replace the hero raster with a live HTML document that shows on mobile. Command: shape.
3. **[P1] Navigation disagrees with the page.** Link order differs from page order (Navbar.tsx:8-13); active state is computed in link order, so "How it works" stays lit on Features; no Book a demo in the mobile header or menu. Fix: reorder links, compute active section by DOM position, add a mobile CTA. Command: polish.
4. **[P1] Viewport-height font scaling makes text tiny on common laptops.** 12.5px root at 1366x650, demo captions about 8px; slate-400 helper text about 2.6:1; browser zoom largely suppressed; Problem padded with about 280px of empty space at 1440x900. Fix: raise the floor to 14px or gate one-screen fitting to min-height 760px; 12px minimum text; replace slate-400; let short sections be short. Command: adapt.
5. **[P2] No clear primary action; no proof or reassurance.** All buttons are the same glass pill; 8 actions on the first screen including two identical Book a demo buttons; three CTAs lead to one form; no logos, rating or data-residency note; pricing is a non-answer. Fix: solid primary Book a demo, secondary as text links; proof strip under the hero; reassurance block beside the form; indicative pricing. Command: clarify (bolder for CTA hierarchy).

## Persona red flags
- **Jordan (first-timer):** h1 is only the product name; the demo shows a US invoice; unexplained jargon ("Merge logic", "Not Required", "Loops for repeating rows"); no price or social proof.
- **Riley (stress tester):** empty submit is not announced or focused; `a@b.c` accepted; long name breaks the layout; fake success; focus lost to body; demo caption re-announced every 2-4s with no pause.
- **Casey (mobile, one-handed):** no hero visual and about 200px of empty gradient; no header CTA; 10px demo dots and 19px footer links; form at the end of a 7-screen page.

## Minor observations
- "How QuillStudio Works" is Title Case with a gradient word; everything else is sentence case.
- The magnetic cursor hides the system cursor.
- The hero raster's baked-in title duplicates the h1 and can't be translated.
- shine-ink and shine-rose headline animations loop forever.
- Faded corner icons on feature cards add texture but no information.
- Footer "Contact" and "Book a demo" both go to #enquiry.

## Questions to consider
- Why is the biggest hero text the product name rather than the promise?
- What would it take to demo one INR cost sheet from template to approved to locked PDF?
- Is one-section-per-screen serving the reader or the layout?
